FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an order take?

It depends on the complexity of your project. Simple transcriptions may take 1–3 days, while original compositions typically take 1–3 weeks. I'll give you a clear estimate after we discuss your brief.
Tergantung kompleksitas proyek. Transkripsi sederhana 1–3 hari, komposisi orisinal 1–3 minggu. Estimasi pasti diberikan setelah diskusi.

Can I use the music commercially?

Yes, commercial licensing is available. Please mention this before ordering so we can adjust the terms and pricing accordingly.
Ya, lisensi komersial tersedia. Harap sebutkan ini sebelum memesan.

What file formats do you deliver?

It depends on the service:
Audio → WAV or MP3  ·  MIDI → .mid  ·  Sheet music / tab → PDF
Please specify your preferred format when ordering.
Audio: WAV/MP3 · MIDI: .mid · Partitur/Tab: PDF. Sebutkan format yang diinginkan saat memesan.

Do you accept rush orders?

Rush orders are possible depending on my current workload and may incur an additional fee. Please contact me first to check availability.
Pesanan kilat bisa dipertimbangkan tergantung workload saya dan mungkin ada biaya tambahan.

I'm a beginner — can I still commission you?

Absolutely! You don't need to be a music expert. Just describe what you're imagining — a mood, a reference song, a story — and I'll take it from there.
Tentu! Kamu tidak perlu jadi ahli musik. Cukup ceritakan mood, referensi, atau cerita yang kamu bayangkan.
