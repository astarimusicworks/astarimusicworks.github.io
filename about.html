Hello, I'm Astari.

I'm a music producer and composer specializing in J-Pop and J-Rock. My musical journey is rooted in a background in Acoustical Physics — a field that taught me to hear music not just as art, but as science. Understanding how sound behaves in space has made me a more intentional and precise producer.

I'm a multi-instrumentalist — I play drums, guitar, bass, piano, and violin. This hands-on relationship with multiple instruments shapes everything I produce. I write parts that feel natural and human, even when they're built inside a DAW.

My released singles — Senjata Terkuat and Narasi Harapan — are available on major streaming platforms. They reflect my love for melodic storytelling and the emotional depth found in Japanese music.

I work under the name Astari — a space where sound meets intention, and every note serves a purpose. Whether you need a transcription, a full original composition, or something in between, I'm here to bring your musical vision to life.
